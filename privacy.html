Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Known Z ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use knownz.co (the "Site"), why we collect it, and how it's handled.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse, create an account, or place an order, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Shipping and billing address
  • Order history and items purchased
  • Device and browser information, collected automatically for basic site functioning

We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details. Payments are processed entirely by Razorpay, our payment gateway partner, under their own security standards and privacy policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer support responses
  • To improve the Site and our product offering
  • To send occasional marketing updates, only if you've opted in (e.g. via our newsletter)

3. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only with the third parties needed to fulfil your order:

  • Razorpay — to process payments securely
  • Shipping/courier partners — to deliver your order to your address

We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Cookies

The Site may use basic cookies to remember your cart and preferences during a session. We don't use cookies for third-party advertising tracking at this time.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at knownz.co@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email knownz.co@gmail.com or reach us on Instagram @knownz.co.

This policy is a general template intended to cover standard practices for a small D2C apparel brand operating in India. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as legal advice. Consider a professional legal review once your store is operating at scale.
