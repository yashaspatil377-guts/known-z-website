Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Where We Ship

We currently ship across India only. International shipping isn't available yet — follow us on Instagram for updates if that changes.

2. Processing Time

Orders are processed and handed to our shipping partner within 1–3 business days of payment confirmation. During drop launches or sales, processing may take slightly longer — we'll flag this on the Site if it does.

3. Delivery Timelines

RegionEstimated Delivery
Metro cities3–5 business days
Other cities & towns5–8 business days
Remote / rural areas7–10 business days

These are estimates from the date of dispatch, not order date, and can vary with courier delays, weather, or regional restrictions beyond our control.

4. Shipping Charges

  • Free shipping on orders above ₹1999
  • A flat ₹99 shipping fee applies to orders below that

Any applicable shipping charge is shown at checkout before you pay.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email/SMS. You can also email knownz.co@gmail.com with your order number for a status update.

6. Delays

While we work with reliable courier partners, occasional delays can happen due to weather, regional logistics issues, or high-demand periods. We'll do our best to keep you updated if your order is running behind.

7. Undelivered / Returned-to-Origin Shipments

If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address or repeated failed delivery attempts, we'll reach out to arrange re-shipping (additional shipping charges may apply) or process a refund minus original shipping costs.

8. Contact Us

Shipping questions? Email knownz.co@gmail.com or message us on Instagram @knownz.co.
