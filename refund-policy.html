Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Known Z is currently in its early launch phase. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order, as our return terms are more limited than they will be once we're fully established.

1. Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order for a full refund any time before it has been shipped. Once an order is marked as shipped, it can no longer be cancelled.

To cancel, email knownz.co@gmail.com with your order number as soon as possible.

2. No Returns or Exchanges (For Now)

As we're just starting out, we're currently not offering returns, exchanges, or refunds for reasons like sizing, change of mind, or personal preference. All sales are final once an order has shipped, outside of the exception below.

We know this is stricter than most established brands, and we're working toward offering size exchanges as we grow. Until then, please double-check our size details before ordering, and feel free to email us at knownz.co@gmail.com if you're unsure what size to pick — we're happy to help before you order.

3. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

This is the one case where we do offer a refund or replacement. If you receive an item that is:

  • Damaged in transit
  • Defective (printing errors, fabric faults, etc.)
  • Not what you ordered

...email us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the issue and your order number. Once verified, we'll cover return shipping and either replace the item or issue a full refund — your choice.

4. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days of approval. Depending on your bank, it may take a few extra days to reflect.

5. This Policy Will Evolve

As Known Z grows, we plan to introduce a more flexible returns and exchange policy. Any changes will be posted here with an updated date.

6. Contact Us

For any cancellation, damaged item, or order issue, email knownz.co@gmail.com with your order number. We aim to respond within 24–48 hours.

This is a general template. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and should not be treated as legal advice. Note that Indian consumer protection law still requires remedies for defective or misrepresented goods regardless of a "final sale" policy — the defective-item clause above is not optional and should not be removed.
